How many walls are there?

Every logo wall is on a family's home. The family keeps the area clean, learns about Bitcoin in the Painted Shacks app and is paid in sats.

What do families agree to?

Every family with a logo wall signs a yearly agreement, written in English and isiXhosa. The program lead and the ward councillor co-sign it.

The family agrees to

  • Have the logo wall painted on their home.
  • Keep the area around the home clean.
  • Finish the week's Bitcoin lesson and quiz in the Painted Shacks app, in isiXhosa, in their own time.

The family receives

  • A payment in sats for each finished week.
  • A freshly painted wall each year.

How this page stays up to date

Straight from the Painted Shacks apps

  • Walls: each shack and its partner in the admin app. Changes show here within a minute.
  • Money in: partner payments recorded in the admin app (Sponsors, Add payment).
  • Money out: the monthly Lightning payouts to families, sent from the admin app.
  • Learning: lessons and quizzes finished in the owner app.
  • Not in the apps yet (shown from the draft): costs, money held, signed agreements, payment proof links, post reach and wall photos.
